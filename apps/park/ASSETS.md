# Park assets

- `public/art/park-dawn-overlook.webp`, `park-evening-boulevard.webp`,
  `park-garden-arrival.webp`: dedicated full-width hero backgrounds. The separate
  `park-flower-market.webp` belongs only to the story section. Each has an 800px
  variant. Created with the built-in image generation tool on 23 October 2026;
  exact prompts are recorded in [HERO-ART-PROMPTS.md](HERO-ART-PROMPTS.md).

- `public/art/park-cafe.webp` and `public/art/park-coast.webp`: original built-in
  image-generation watercolours for the illustrated journal carousel, created on
  23 October 2026. Full prompts are recorded in [ART-PROMPTS.md](ART-PROMPTS.md).
  Responsive `-800.webp` variants are optimized for mobile. The neighbourhood
  illustration also has an 800px variant. These are decorative imaginary scenes.

- `public/art/park-neighbourhood.webp`: original AI-generated watercolour street illustration,
  created with the built-in image generation tool on 23 October 2026. Optimized as WebP
  for the parking walkthrough. It is decorative artwork, not a customer's
  parking location or vehicle photo. Final prompt: “A premium editorial watercolour
  illustration for VaahanSafe Park, a private parking notebook website in India.
  Wide landscape composition, approximately 16:9. A calm leafy Indian neighbourhood
  street in warm afternoon sunlight, a small ivory hatchback parked legally at the
  curb beside a low terracotta garden wall and flowering bougainvillea, a parked muted
  green scooter farther along the lane, trees casting delicate dappled shadows, a
  pedestrian couple walking away in the mid-distance, no recognizable faces.
  Hand-painted transparent watercolour and fine graphite on warm ivory cotton paper,
  soft olive greens, warm ochre, dusty clay, generous airy sky and subtle paper grain.
  Sophisticated travel-journal editorial art, peaceful, timeless, natural proportions
  and restrained detail. No text, no typography, no badges, no UI, no map pins, no logos,
  no license plate letters or numbers, no border. This is decorative marketing
  artwork, not a real user parking photo.”

- `public/art/park-hero.webp`: original AI-generated editorial watercolour illustration, created for VaahanSafe Park on 22 October 2026. Decorative marketing illustration, not a customer's vehicle or parking record.
- `public/art/paper-texture.webp`: existing VaahanSafe paper texture reused from the viewer.
- `public/fonts/*`: existing Cormorant Garamond and Inter subsets; license files copied alongside the fonts.
- Hugeicons free vectors: existing `@hugeicons/core-free-icons` package and serializer.
- `public/favicon.svg`: original code-drawn Park monogram and location pin. Touch,
  install and maskable PNG variants are rasterized by `generate-park-icons.mjs`.
