# Activation paper artwork

Created with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-10-03. Original PNGs remain in the Codex generated image directory. The app uses transparent WebP versions at 720 × 480; all three total approximately 100 KB. Next Image loads these supporting illustrations lazily with responsive sizes.

These are decorative illustrations, never QR credentials or activation status. They contain no codes, personal data, or usable QR patterns.

## Final prompt set

- **Proof:** Premium flat 2D paper collage of an ivory scratch card with a silver strip, terracotta key, and sage shield with a check. Editorial hand-cut paper, restrained shading and tactile grain. Warm ivory, terracotta, sage, charcoal. Compact composition, genuine transparent background. No text, digits, QR code, logo, watermark, or glossy 3D.
- **Mobile:** Matching 2D paper collage of a charcoal phone, cream message card with a sage check, and terracotta seal. Clean silhouette, paper grain and subtle overlapping layers. Transparent alpha, no colored backdrop, text, digits, QR code, logo, watermark, or personal data.
- **Vehicle:** Matching 2D paper collage of a sage hatchback, ivory vehicle identity card with an unlettered car symbol and lines, terracotta connecting ribbon, and cream check seal. Transparent alpha, paper grain and restrained layer shadows. No text, registration contents, QR code, logo, watermark, or surrounding road scene.
